## How the additions affect the study position

- Resource optimization, self-adaptation, topology-aware scheduling and checkpoint/recovery design are established areas. EcoStream-Agent cannot claim novelty for scaling, adaptation, or transition-cost awareness alone.
- Multimodal observability and non-agentic failure diagnosis are established. The relevant comparison is sequential action-sensitive evidence acquisition versus fixed multimodal fusion, deterministic rules, numerical control and a conventional diagnostic agent.
- Benchmarking evidence reinforces that framework, partitioning, serialization, state and network conditions must be reported; controller effects cannot be inferred from a single engine result without qualification.
- The candidate contribution remains a decision rule that values an observation by its expected effect on a feasible intervention after evidence, delay and transition costs. This remains a hypothesis until implemented and tested.
